# Synthetic data, PII and PCI disclosure

This dataset was purpose-built for an offline defensive training exercise. It contains **no real PII**, **no real PCI/cardholder data**, no actual employee identity, and no third-party copyrighted incident image or customer dataset. Hostnames (`nb-*`), usernames/principals (`NB\*`), change identifiers, content inside the compressed archive, and all audit events were authored as invented fixtures.

The archive contains simulated forecasting records and a readme. They are fake internal business data to provide a realistic protected-data target; none refers to actual customers or individuals. Background events were added as synthetic scheduled health checks, routine status GET requests, service tokens and backup jobs to prevent a toy-only malicious dataset.

External indicators use documentation-only prefixes `192.0.2.0/24` and `198.51.100.0/24` and reserved `.example` names, and must not be treated as real-world indicators of compromise. The detached Ed25519 signature and training public key were generated for this fabricated case and are not third-party attestation.

All generated materials are educational. There is no sample malware and therefore no malware password requirement. Publishers may choose a separate downloadable-archive access password subject to the receiving platform's distribution process.
